About Nb.CA Academy

From Coffee Cherry To Cup

Nb.CA Academy is short for Nine Bar Coffee Academy. We are located in Sungai Petani, Kedah Darul Aman.

Nb.CA provides Coffee Education Workshops & Training Programmes to coffee enthusiasts — even without prior experience.

Our Coffee Programs take you on a journey from Coffee Cherry all the way to a wonderful cup of coffee, beginning with the Coffee History, Farms, Coffee Cherry, and Coffee Processes, through to how the Green Beans are Shipped, Roasted and Packed.

As for Barista and Home Brewing Enthusiasts, we'll help you understand the key Sensory Skills and different Coffee Flavour Profiles. You'll also learn Barista Skills such as Hand Brew & Latte Art technique.

Our Vision

We aim to become Malaysia's go-to coffee education academy. We want every student to leave with real, usable skill.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple—to deliver consistent, high-quality, hands-on coffee classes. We're committed to small class sizes and real practice time.
