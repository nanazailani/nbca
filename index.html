About Rumah Panggang

Our Journey & Coffee Story

Welcome to Rumah Panggang! We started in 2019, and despite the slow beginnings during the COVID-19 MCO phase, things picked up in 2021 with the growing café scene.

Based in Sungai Petani, Kedah, we're known for roasting the freshest coffee beans. Our dedicated roaster, Iman Roslan, puts heart and skill into every batch.

We're proud to be a major supplier to cafes in the North, serving coffee lovers all around. At Rumah Panggang, it's not just about coffee. It's about sharing an amazing cup and building a community around our love for great brews.

Come join us in this aromatic adventure—from our roaster's hands to your cup. Discover the goodness of Rumah Panggang!

Our Vision

We aim to become the go-to coffee roaster in Malaysia. We want to offer the best quality and taste in every cup.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple—to provide you with freshly roasted coffee beans consistently. We're committed to bringing you top-notch beans bursting with freshness and flavor.

Meet The Roaster

Iman Roslan

Head Roaster & Storyteller

Meet Iman Roslan, the driving force behind Rumah Panggang's exceptional coffee journey. He started as a barista and ran a café but felt a strong pull to dive deeper into the world of coffee.

With a desire to explore more, Iman shifted gears and focused on roasting coffee. At Rumah Panggang, he's the wizard behind the roaster, carefully choosing the best beans and using his know-how to bring out their amazing flavors.

Iman isn't just roasting beans; he's creating an experience. Every batch tells a story of taste and smell, showing his dedication to making the perfect cup. He's not just a roaster; he's a storyteller, sharing the joy of great coffee with every bean he roasts.
